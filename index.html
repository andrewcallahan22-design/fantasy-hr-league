Live Sync

League data is shared — every edit and sync updates the same scoreboard for all managers. The sync pulls official MLB totals; when a rostered player's season total rises, the new homers are added to their month count and logged in History. Your entered numbers are the baseline — the sync only adds what's new.

✓ Auto-sync runs on the server at 5 PM and 11 PM Pacific — nobody needs to keep the page open. The ⟳ Sync button triggers it immediately for the whole league.
